# Sleep Budget 🌙

A 2D game about a programmer's day: plan your time yourself, set the alarm, catch the bus, and don't forget your badge. Tonight's choices decide tomorrow morning, and the morning decides the whole day.

**🚧 Work in progress.** You can't play it yet, but you can follow along as it's being built from scratch.

## About

You're an ordinary office programmer. You walk around your apartment, commute to work, sit at your computer, and survive meetings. Time is the main resource: every action costs minutes, and the sleep you skipped last night will catch up with you in the morning and throughout the day.
